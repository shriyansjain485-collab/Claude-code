## Sources and rights

A clip is only made from a video listed here or fetched with one of these bases.
"It's on YouTube" is not a basis; neither is "fair use" on its own.

| Basis | Meaning | Proof kept in the draft |
|---|---|---|
| `own` | You made the video | none needed |
| `permission` | The creator said yes to you | who, when, where (email, DM, contract) |
| `licensed` | Creator runs a public clipping programme, or you bought a licence | link to the terms |
| `cc-by` | YouTube lists the video as Creative Commons (checked automatically) | credit in the description |
